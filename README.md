### Personal Statement on Ethics
My view on ethics is shaped by these core values:
- Privacy: User data is always private and cannot be viewed or used maliciously.
- Transparency: Users will always be appropriately informed on what and how systems work and decisions are made.
- Accountability: I and every member of my company will take responsibility.
- Integrity: Products are designed with honesty and the purpose to create unique solutions to practical problems.

Ethics are a core part of the development process, not an afterthought. Technical ability also comes with responsibility, and I ensure to uphold these ethics in my work going forward.
